In short

On 183 questions I wrote alongside the engine, GlassBox answered or refused correctly every time (95% CI 97.9–100%). A development set, not an independent benchmark.1

Problem

Advisors answer clients under suitability rules. A confident wrong answer is a compliance problem, and a chat assistant can’t show where an answer came from. GlassBox answers only from a client’s approved documents and refuses everything else.

How an answer is made

  1. 1Advisor asks about a client
  2. 2Retrieve approved documents only
  3. 3Draft the answer as claims, and score each one
  4. Supported: answer, every claim cited
  5. Unsupported: refuse, naming the missing source
  6. Either way: appended to a hash-chained audit log; compliance reviews flags
