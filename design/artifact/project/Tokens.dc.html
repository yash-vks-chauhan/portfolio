06 · Mark

Dot-matrix monogram

“YC” on an 11 × 7 grid of 5 × 7 glyphs: on-dots in currentColor, off-dots in --line. Used in the status bar (44 × 28) and as the favicon. The only dot-matrix element on the site; it ripples once on hover.
