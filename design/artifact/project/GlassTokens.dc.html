Motion

  • Springs for sheets, segmented controls and the dock: stiffness 300, damping 30 (Motion).
  • Lift on cards: 4px over 500 ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1).
  • One live moment per screen: the hero background. Counters roll once, in view.
  • Reduced motion: a still frame replaces WebGL, numbers appear final, no tilt or parallax.
